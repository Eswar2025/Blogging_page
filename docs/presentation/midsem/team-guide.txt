What to do
Download DBMS_Midsem_Slides.pptx and Slide_Images.zip. Unzip the images.
In Canva, open Uploads, upload the .pptx file, then click it to open it as an editable design. Every slide, table box, and text box comes in editable.
On slide 1, replace every [bracket] with your names, roll numbers, and the professor's or TA's name.
If you want a different look, change only colors and fonts (Canva's Styles panel does it for all slides at once). Keep every slide and keep the order.
Do not rename any table or column. Names like post_revision and parent_comment_id must match the report exactly, underscores included.
Keep the ER diagram (slide 3) and the final schema (slide 17) as one whole image each, as large as the slide allows. If they look blurry after import, delete them and drag in the HD versions from Slide_Images.zip.
You can shorten a sentence if a box feels crowded, but don't change what it says. Every line is something we may be asked about.
Check every slide in Present mode. All text should be readable from the back of a classroom.
Share the Canva link with the team (access: Can edit) and download a PDF copy as a backup.
Don't add animations, stock photos, or AI-generated pictures. The panel is grading the database design, and a plain deck keeps their attention on it.
